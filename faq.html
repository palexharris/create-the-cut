About the event

What is Create the Cut?
A haircutting competition for Northeast Ohio cosmetology and barbering students. Instructor-nominated students compete through a series of timed haircuts on mannequins, and the top three win cash prizes — with a matching gift to each winner’s school.
Who runs it?
The independently owned and operated Great Clips salons of Cleveland-Akron-Canton. Local franchisees and their salon teams plan, staff and fund the entire event.
When and where is the 2027 competition?
Early March 2027. The exact date and venue will be announced soon — join the interest list and you’ll be the first to know. Past competitions were held on a Sunday afternoon in early March.
How long does it last?
Plan on an afternoon. Past events ran from about 1:00 to 4:30 p.m., with competitor check-in starting around 12:30.
Can family and friends come watch?
Yes! Spectators are welcome — no sign-up needed — and the cheering section is a big part of the fun.

Competing

Who is eligible?
Students currently enrolled in — or recently graduated from — a cosmetology or barbering program in Northeast Ohio, who are nominated by an instructor at their school.
How do I enter?
Competitors are nominated by their instructors. Fill out the student interest form and let your instructor know you want to compete. Instructors can sign up here.
How many students can each school send?
In past years each program could nominate up to two students. The 2027 limit will be shared when nominations open.
Does it cost anything to compete?
No — there is no entry fee.
What will I have to cut?
Competitors work on mannequins and complete timed haircuts in sequence — a long cut, a medium cut and a short cut — with roughly 15–20 minutes for each.
How is it judged?
A panel of judges circulates while competitors work and scores each finished look. The highest scores take 1st, 2nd and 3rd.
What do I need to bring?
Confirmed competitors receive a packet with the rules, supply list and check-in details before the event.
I’m under 18 — can I still compete?
Yes. Every competitor signs an Affidavit of Eligibility & Release, and a parent or guardian also signs if you’re under 18.

Prizes

What are the prizes?
1st place: $1,000 · 2nd place: $750 · 3rd place: $500. Each winner’s school receives a matching gift of the same amount.
Can I take tools instead of cash?
Yes. Winners choose either the cash prize or a professional tool package of equal value to help start their career. Schools can likewise take their matching gift as cash or as tools for their program.
How much has been awarded so far?
More than $9,000 has gone to student winners and their schools across recent competitions — all funded by local Great Clips franchisees.

Schools & more

I’m an instructor. How do I get my program involved?
Sign up on the interest form and we’ll send nomination details as soon as they’re available.
Will there be photos and video?
Yes. The event is professionally photographed and filmed, and local media have covered past competitions. See the gallery and news pages.
Does competing lead to a job at Great Clips?
There’s no obligation, but many franchisees are hiring and love meeting new talent. Explore openings at jobs.greatclips.com.

Ready to show what you can do?

Get on the list for Create the Cut 2027 — we’ll email nominations, dates and details the moment they’re announced.
