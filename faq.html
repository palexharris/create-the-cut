FAQs

Questions? Answers.

Can’t find what you need? Send us a note on the sign-up form.

About the event

What is Create the Cut?
A haircutting competition for Northeast Ohio cosmetology students. Instructor-nominated students compete through two timed rounds on mannequins, and the top three win tools or tuition money — with a matching gift to each winner’s school.
Who runs it?
The independently owned and operated Great Clips salons of Cleveland-Akron-Canton. Local franchisees and their salon teams plan, staff and fund the entire event.
When and where is the 2027 competition?
Sunday, March 14, 2027, at the Don Umerley Civic Center, 21016 Hilliard Blvd, Rocky River, OH 44116. Add it to your calendar and join the interest list for nomination details and the start time.
How long does it last?
Plan on an afternoon. In 2026, check-in opened at noon, the competition started at 1:00 and winners were announced around 3:20.
Can family and friends come watch?
Yes! Spectators are welcome — no sign-up needed. Light refreshments are provided, and there’s trivia with giveaways for the crowd while competitors cut. The cheering section is a big part of the fun.

Competing

Who is eligible?
Only current cosmetology students — currently enrolled in a Northeast Ohio cosmetology program and nominated by an instructor at their school. Graduates are not eligible.
How do I enter?
Students can’t enter themselves — all nominations must come from cosmetology school instructors. Fill out the student interest form so we know you’re interested, then let your instructor know you want to compete. Instructors can sign up here to receive the nomination form.
Does the student interest form enter me in the competition?
No. The student form only tells us you’re interested so we can keep you posted. To compete, you must be nominated by your cosmetology school instructor.
How many students can each school send?
Each program may nominate up to two students.
Does it cost anything to compete?
No — there is no entry fee.
What will I have to cut?
Competitors work on mannequins through two timed rounds of about 40 minutes each: Round 1 is a shear cut and style, and Round 2 is a fade of the competitor’s choice.
How is it judged?
A panel of judges scores each round right after time is called. Combined scores decide 1st, 2nd and 3rd.
What do I need to bring?
Confirmed competitors receive a packet with the rules, supply list and check-in details before the event.
I’m under 18 — can I still compete?
Yes. Every competitor signs an Affidavit of Eligibility & Release, and a parent or guardian also signs if you’re under 18.

Prizes

What are the prizes?
1st place: $1,000 · 2nd place: $750 · 3rd place: $500. Each winner’s school receives a matching gift of the same amount.
Is the prize paid in cash?
No. Prizes go toward the winner’s tuition, or — if they don’t owe tuition — come as a professional tool package of equal value to help start their career. Each school’s matching gift is a check or tools for their program.
How much has been awarded so far?
More than $16,000 has gone to student winners and their schools across recent competitions — all funded by local Great Clips franchisees.

Schools & more

I’m an instructor. How do I get my program involved?
Sign up on the interest form and we’ll email you the nomination form as soon as nominations open. Nominations are accepted from instructors only.
Will there be photos and video?
Yes. The event is professionally photographed and filmed, and local media have covered past competitions. See the gallery and news pages.
Does competing lead to a job at Great Clips?
There’s no obligation, but many franchisees are hiring and love meeting new talent. Explore openings at jobs.greatclips.com.

Ready to show what you can do?

Students: let us know you’re interested. Instructors: sign up to receive the nomination form — all competitors are nominated by their cosmetology school instructors.
