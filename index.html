What is Create the Cut?

Two cuts. One clock. Real stakes.

Create the Cut gives the region’s most promising stylists a stage. Instructors nominate their top students, who then compete on mannequins through two timed rounds — a haircut and style, then a second haircut — while a panel of judges scores their technique and finished looks.

Family, friends and instructors pack the room to cheer them on, and the top three walk away with oversized checks, bragging rights and a head start on their careers.
